## Notation

### scalar-vector-matrix-tensor

Italic, bold lower-case and bold upper-case letters denote scalars, vectors and matrices, respectively.

| Types       | Latex                    |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Scalar (标量) | $ a, n, \lambda $          |
| Vector (向量) | $ \mathbf{a}, \mathbf{x} $ |
| Matrix (矩阵) | $ \mathbf{X} $ |
| Tensor (张量) | $ \mathcal{X} $ |
